THE PROBLEM

Farm irrigation is a water problem
AND an energy problem.

  • When to irrigate? Farmers must judge timing from weather, soil moisture, and crop stage—often separately.
  • How much water? Fixed schedules miss changing field conditions, leading to over- or under-irrigation.
  • Energy cost: Pumping consumes electricity or diesel. Tariff timing and solar availability are rarely considered alongside water need.
  • Fragmented signals: Weather, soil, crop demand, water status, and energy cost are treated as disconnected datasets.
  • No unified recommendation: Farmers need one actionable decision—not five dashboards.

THE SCIENCE

Built on agricultural physics—not a black box.

  • FAO-56 Penman-Monteith — deterministic reference evapotranspiration (ET₀) from net radiation, vapor pressure deficit, wind speed, and psychrometric constants.
  • Saxton-Rawls pedotransfer — converts SoilGrids texture (sand%, clay%) into field capacity (θFC), wilting point (θWP), and available water capacity.
  • Root-zone water balance — daily depletion mass model tracking Dr against USDA-SCS effective rainfall and adjusted ETc.
  • Crop water stress — Kₛ coefficient and CWSI derived from depletion vs. readily-available water thresholds.
  • Effective rainfall — USDA-SCS methodology separating surface runoff from infiltrating precipitation.
FAO-56 Penman-Monteith Reference ET₀
ET₀ = [0.408·Δ·(Rn − G) + γ·(900/(T+273))·u₂·(eₛ − eₐ)]
            / [Δ + γ·(1 + 0.34·u₂)]
Root-Zone Depletion & Stress
Dr,i = Dr,i−1 − Peff − I + ETc,adj
CWSI = 1 − Kₛ

THE ENERGY INNOVATION

Water intelligence meets solar intelligence.

Instead of treating irrigation demand and energy cost as separate decisions, KISAN URJA connects them. The platform models when solar PV generation is sufficient to power irrigation pumps—synchronizing water delivery with available renewable energy.
DECISION FUSION
💧 Crop Water Need + 🪨 Soil Water Status + ☀️ Solar Availability + ⚙️ Pump Capacity + 💰 Energy Cost / Tariff → OPTIMAL IRRIGATION WINDOW
Demo profiles model savings scenarios (e.g., ₹94,200/yr for a 5HP solar pump in Haryana). These represent modeled scenarios, not independently measured field results.
